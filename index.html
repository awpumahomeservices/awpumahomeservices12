Home comfort in Central Massachusetts

Heating and cooling services for Worcester homes

Worcester's housing ranges from historic homes to newer construction. Each property has different heating, cooling and electrical needs. A.W. Puma Home Services offers solutions to help homeowners plan repairs, replacements and equipment upgrades.

♨

Heat Pump Installation & Service

Heat pumps provide heating and cooling in one system. They may be a practical option when replacing older oil, propane or electric heating equipment. System selection should account for the home's layout and existing equipment.

❄

Ductless Mini-Splits

Ductless systems can help condition spaces without existing ductwork, including older homes, finished basements, converted attics, additions and rooms with uneven temperatures.

⌂

Furnace Installation & Repair

Unusual noises, uneven temperatures, frequent cycling and rising energy use can signal a furnace problem. A professional inspection can help identify whether repair or replacement makes sense.

✳

Air Conditioning

Central air conditioning installation and service can help maintain comfortable indoor temperatures during warm Massachusetts summers. Proper equipment selection and installation matter.

▤

Boilers & Water Heaters

Boilers remain common in Massachusetts homes. A.W. Puma advertises boiler repair, replacement and maintenance, along with traditional and tankless water heater solutions.

⚡

Electrical Panel Upgrades

New HVAC equipment may require more electrical capacity than an older panel can provide. A.W. Puma lists an in-house licensed electrical crew for panel upgrades and publishes Massachusetts electrical license number 9116-EL-A1.
